# Museum artifact generation specification

Generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow. Each asset uses this shared direction:

> Photorealistic luxury product photograph of a museum-grade miniature on a short square honed black-stone plinth. Portrait 4:5 framing, fixed eye-level camera slightly above the plinth, 50mm lens feel, centered three-quarter view, warm amber key and rim light from upper left, faint cool fill from right, deep cinematic contrast, black/charcoal/brushed-metal palette. No glass enclosure, labels, project names, typography, logos, watermarks, people, or floor beyond the plinth.

Subject prompts:

1. **Loomis US** — Generic black-and-brushed-silver armored cash-security truck, three-quarter front view facing slightly left, true transparent alpha backdrop.
2. **ABS Wavesight** — Generic white, charcoal, and brushed-metal commercial maritime work vessel with a compact superstructure and working deck, three-quarter bow view, seamless near-black studio backdrop.
3. **Eleox** — Symmetrical natural-gas pipeline assembly with two thick curved black-steel pipes, a central bronze valve body, and a handwheel, seamless near-black studio backdrop.
4. **Pomodoro Champion** — Round matte-black and brushed-silver mechanical stopwatch with crown, pushers, and non-numeric tick marks, upright on a discreet stand, seamless near-black studio backdrop.
5. **Adcetera** — Original abstract A-like architectural sculpture made from intersecting brushed-aluminum and matte-charcoal planes, seamless near-black studio backdrop; an art object, not a logo.
6. **Chevron** — Compact generic refinery model with three distillation towers, pipe racks, heat exchangers, tanks, and catwalks, seamless near-black studio backdrop; no fire or smoke.

The Loomis render served as the reference image for camera, lighting, plinth proportions, and scale across the remaining set.
